## 👋 About

I'm a high school student at **Institut Le Rosey** (Rolle, Switzerland), in the IB Diploma programme, class of 2027. I build education software, compete in programming and robotics, contribute to research on AI-based pragmatics assessment, and play the violin.

- 🏫 Founder of **Studious** (a modern LMS, live since 2023) and co-founder of **Scribe**
- 🥇 1st Place Overall, **HackHarvard China 2025** · 🥉 3rd Place (Education Track) + Outstanding Impact Award, **HackMIT China 2026**
- 💻 **USACO Gold** (1000/1000 in Silver, direct promotion)
- 🤖 VEX **15520X**: Excellence Award at Alpine Robo Games 2026, qualified for **VEX Worlds** in Dallas
- 🎓 Summer 2026: **Penn ESAP**, AI and its mathematics
