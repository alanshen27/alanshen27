### hey, i'm alan

student in rolle, switzerland. i mostly build stuff for students (founder of <a href="https://www.studious.sh">studious</a> and <a href="https://scribe.study">scribe</a>), but most of my repos are side quests: games, tiny tools, ml toys, and whatever i got curious about that week.

### currently tinkering with

- <a href="https://github.com/alanshen27/museblet"><b>museblet</b></a> (aka nocturne): a full-body instrument. stand in front of the camera, slow hands brush a guqin, a fast fist lands a strike, a kick tears red ink across the screen. pentatonic modes and stone-rubbing visuals.
- <a href="https://github.com/alanshen27/aviata"><b>aviata</b></a>: spots private-jet empty legs from ads-b data with xgboost + an lstm.
- <a href="https://github.com/alanshen27/responder"><b>responder</b></a>: a 3d 911-dispatch game. callers lie, fires spread, the budget bleeds.
- <a href="https://ffnn-viz.vercel.app"><b>ffnn-viz</b></a>: watch a feed-forward neural net do its thing.
